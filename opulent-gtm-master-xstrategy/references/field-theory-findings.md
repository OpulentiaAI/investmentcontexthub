# Field Theory Findings

## Corpus method

Field Theory CLI v1.3.22 was queried through 58 search lenses across GTM, sales, marketing, outbound, enrichment, CRM, pricing, discovery, demos, follow-up, partnerships, PLG, sales engineering, customer success, and related terms. The local corpus contained 869 bookmarks dated 2024-07-16 through 2026-07-27; the union contained 178 unique GTM and sales candidates. Treat social-post metrics and case claims as hypotheses until independently verified.

## Durable findings

### Make GTM a compounding context system

Ramp's GTM coworker pattern emphasizes shared account context, short episodes, triggers, memory, evals, approval points, and write-back. The moat is institutional learning, not the agent alone.

Source: https://x.com/ParthGujare_/status/2080716441041465427

### Narrow the enterprise wedge

The Notion EDP example argues that a specific team-level wedge can improve win rates, deal size, sales cycle, and organic expansion better than a broad enterprise ICP.

Source: https://x.com/Prerrrrna_/status/2076678433904755134

### Research and useful ideas beat generic pitching

Creative-idea outreach proposes two or three concrete growth ideas before introducing the seller. Pre-call enrichment should arrive before every meeting and eliminate manual research.

Sources:

- https://x.com/chrispisarski/status/2077880639055941693
- https://x.com/fivosaresti/status/2072032494912704777

### Use underused public intent signals

Funding, contracts, job postings, technology adoption, filings, reviews, event participation, and active social engagement can be fresher and less saturated than purchased static lists.

Sources:

- https://x.com/scaling_shields/status/2018594563594846557
- https://x.com/draprints/status/2019410722137198655
- https://x.com/Kazanjy/status/2032893350597087330

### Encode research as skills and versioned systems

The Browserbase company-research skill and GTMbrain examples treat GTM workflows as forkable, inspectable systems rather than private operator intuition.

Sources:

- https://x.com/JaySahnan/status/2047730585313980499
- https://x.com/kathrynwu1/status/2041909258988745013

### Treat product proof and trust as channels

When products are easier to build, trust, field evidence, employee expertise, fast shipping, and product experience become primary distribution assets.

Sources:

- https://x.com/HarryStebbings/status/2032847555432730961
- https://x.com/buccocapital/status/2037320833509523471

### Keep the sales motion disciplined

Use a bounded discovery, a relevant demo, concrete commitments, and a multi-touch follow-up sequence. AI should prevent dropped follow-ups, not create more noise.

Sources:

- https://x.com/Kazanjy/status/2043766982852464724
- https://x.com/Kazanjy/status/2017326782161023141
- https://x.com/Kazanjy/status/2017660471084564610
- https://x.com/jasonlk/status/2060878172632105002

### Price the business outcome, then test it

One operator describes paid audits before implementation and warns that small undifferentiated automation projects create coordination-heavy economics. Use this as a pricing hypothesis, not a universal benchmark.

Sources:

- https://x.com/lukepierceops/status/2059606571357720939
- https://x.com/lukepierceops/status/2050545337253675154

## Gap check

The first pass was outbound-heavy. The second pass explicitly added PLG, sales engineering, customer success, sales enablement, referral, case study, product-led, and post-sale terms. The final strategy therefore includes authority, partnerships, sales execution, and retention learning rather than treating GTM as list building alone.
