# Operating Sequence

## Strategy spine

Run one closed loop:

1. Define the commercial decision.
2. Choose one segment-use-case wedge from `opulent-use-case-catalog.md`; retain the source URL and separate observed site labels from editorial GTM labels.
3. Normalize relationship and account data.
4. Detect a current signal or prove a warm relationship.
5. Research the account and create a decision brief.
6. Draft one channel-native action.
7. Obtain approval.
8. Execute, record the effect, and prepare the next commitment.
9. Compare with baseline.
10. Write corrections back into account memory and the playbook.

## 90-day rollout

### Days 0-14: Foundation

- Select one `Primary wedge` or `Partner wedge` from the catalog: default to financial services plus deal-flow or data-analysis workflows, or consulting plus agent-run analysis, unless stronger evidence exists.
- Name the buyer, value motion, lifecycle stage, proof plan, and source URL; treat expansion modules and proof accelerators as support, not parallel offers.
- Establish Brand and Revenue Context, account schema, evidence rules, ownership, exclusions, and approval policy.
- Normalize the Diego workbook ladder; do not start from all raw connections.
- Define the baseline funnel and the first three hypotheses.

Exit gate: every pilot account has a stable identity, fit reason, owner, evidence, and next action.

### Days 15-30: Warm wedge

- Start with 10-15 high-context warm accounts per week.
- Produce a short account brief and a four-part message for each.
- Run human-reviewed LinkedIn or email outreach.
- Pair discovery with one relevant demo or proof artifact.

Exit gate: at least one segment-message-workflow combination shows qualitative pull or measurable lift over the baseline.

### Days 31-50: Signal engine

- Monitor two or three underused public sources per wedge.
- Add freshness and confidence to every signal.
- Generate pre-call briefs automatically after meetings are booked.
- Promote stable research steps from browser traces to typed APIs where practical.

Exit gate: signal-to-brief latency and evidence freshness meet the service-level target; false-positive causes are labeled.

### Days 51-70: Authority and partner motion

- Turn real delivery evidence into founder or employee field notes.
- Package one case proof, one technical explainer, and one point-of-view asset.
- Test one partnership route: agency, integration, referral, platform, affiliate, or event.

Exit gate: content and partner actions originate from real account evidence, not generic editorial calendars.

### Days 71-90: Scale or kill

- Compare wedges, channels, messages, and offers on qualified pipeline and learning speed.
- Kill weak plays, preserve counter-evidence, and promote strong plays into versioned playbooks.
- Expand only one dimension at a time: more accounts, another signal source, another channel, or another segment.

Exit gate: the next 90-day plan has a keep, kill, or revise decision for every active play.

## Weekly cadence

- Monday: refresh evidence and choose the week's hypothesis.
- Tuesday: complete account briefs and approve the target set.
- Wednesday: execute outreach and pre-call preparation.
- Thursday: run discovery, relevant demos, and partner actions.
- Friday: review funnel quality, corrections, commitments, and playbook updates.

## Handoff sequence

Strategy owner -> research/data owner -> campaign lead -> channel specialist -> sales owner -> account memory.

Each handoff is an artifact, not a chat summary. Every artifact includes objective, inputs, evidence, exclusions, owner, deadline, approval state, and expected output.
