# Evidence and Metrics

## Evidence quality

Every fact carries:

- source URL or source artifact
- capture time
- source type
- freshness window
- confidence
- fact versus inference label
- owner

Evidence expires. Job postings and role changes should normally be refreshed within days; strategic filings and durable product pages may last longer. Define the window per source.

## Core scorecard

### Data health

- stable identity coverage
- deduplication rate
- company-domain resolution rate
- work-email verification rate
- segment and use-case completeness
- current-signal coverage
- evidence freshness
- owner coverage

### Research quality

- candidate-to-accepted evidence rate
- unsupported-claim rate
- false-positive rate by source
- time from signal to decision brief
- human correction rate and reason

### Funnel quality

- targeted accounts
- approved contacts
- positive replies
- discovery meetings
- qualified opportunities
- proposals
- wins
- disqualification reasons

Show conversion between adjacent stages and cohort results by segment, signal source, offer, and channel.

### Learning and economics

- experiments completed per week
- time to first reliable learning
- playbook reuse rate
- correction capture rate
- human minutes per accepted account
- human minutes per qualified meeting
- cost per accepted account
- pipeline created per research hour
- payback period for acquired customers

## Experiment card

- Hypothesis
- Baseline
- Single changed variable
- Cohort and sample
- Start and stop dates
- Leading indicator
- Revenue-quality outcome
- Guardrail metric
- Result
- Keep, kill, or revise decision
- Playbook or account-memory update

## Approval checklist

- Identity and recipient verified
- Sender verified
- Relationship or channel basis documented
- Evidence current and attributable
- Message matches evidence
- No sensitive data leakage
- One clear next action
- Stop condition defined
- CRM write-back ready
- Human approver recorded

## Weekly revenue learning report

Lead with decisions, not activity:

1. What changed in the market or account evidence.
2. Which hypothesis was tested.
3. What the funnel and qualitative evidence showed.
4. What was corrected.
5. Which play was kept, killed, or revised.
6. What will change next week.
